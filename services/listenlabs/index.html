About

High-conversion marketing site

For ListenLabs, we developed a fast-loading marketing site designed to convert cold traffic into demo bookings. The site features smooth GSAP animations and a pixel-perfect mobile experience.

Task

Capturing the SaaS market

ListenLabs needed a web presence that matched the quality of their audio-analysis product. Their old site was slow, not mobile-responsive, and failed to communicate their value proposition effectively.

Solution

Performance-first architecture

We built a static site using modern best practices for speed and SEO. By optimizing asset delivery and implementing lazy-loading, we achieved a sub-2-second Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) and a nearly perfect Lighthouse score.
