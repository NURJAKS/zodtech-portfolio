About

Intelligent search companion

For Wonder, we built a search experience powered by AI-Bots. The tool focuses on synthesizing information from diverse sources into a coherent, cited answer, providing users with a faster way to research complex topics.

Task

Organizing the world's data

Wonder needed a way to present search results that went beyond simple links. The goal was to use AI-Bots to understand intent and provide direct answers with supporting evidence, reducing the time spent clicking through multiple tabs.

Solution

Semantic answer engine

We developed a semantic search UI that prioritizes answers over links. By integrating AI-Bots NLP capabilities, we created a system that clusters related information and presents it in a clean, readable format with interactive citations.
