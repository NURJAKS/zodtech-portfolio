About the client:

LMS Platform is a modern platform that helps freelancers and teams manage their work and payments. They focus on transparency and efficiency in the education sector, serving thousands of users worldwide.

What we did:

We built a specialized management layer to handle student qualification and distribution. LMS Platform needed to handle high volumes of inbound requests without increasing the administrative team size. We implemented a series of event-driven workflows that trigger based on student behavior, including automated scoring and instant notifications.
